The air beneath the branches
pools into mist and dew mirrored blue
Seething whispers uttered from featureless faces
Casting off years like leaves into a swelling sea
We the fallen, gather in our hallows

Terror and sleep intertwined
Within the tortured womb... the future's drum beats
We were awakened by the calling
In wonder and amazement,
We watched our blood burn the sky
We chose to be alive

Forked branches cast their own sentient shadows
We hold our breath as the air grows paler
A brief respite, and then we return
to harvesting moments
between nothing and tomorrow

We gathered among those who wept
To witness the grand unveiling
Feeding the earth's sentient seed to the swelling sea
The hands of the weary gently touch my mouth
and I recount their days and the memories of wounds
It was when we were last together
