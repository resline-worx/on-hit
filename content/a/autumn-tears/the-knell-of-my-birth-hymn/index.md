There is no stillness beneath the sun
And I feel brave to have touched the sky
Fading like words... fade into stone
I have come to understand the folly of miracles
Where I can dream... and wake... and die in vain
With the untold truth Resting on my shoulders
(The resounding bell which cannot be unrung)

My blood trickles upon a golden rose
It's petals hold fast against angry winds
The quiet falls... a massive silence as heavy as doom
Time means nothing to me... nothing at all
(I could not spend it, so I gave it all away)

Black shrines as tall as mountains
Towering above the tranquility of the clouds
(Yet beneath all of this.. my slumber keeps)
My thoughts possessed the world for only a moment
Before the Earth drew me back to her bosom
(Serene and majestic is my garden grave)
