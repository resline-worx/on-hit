The days just run together here sometimes
so I went to the opera last night
& I thought I would love it, but I just felt so above it
because it was above me.

I shaved my face & did my hair up nice
& watched a video that helped me tie my tie.
I looked so groovy I could play myself in a movie
& they’d pour me a star on Hollywood Boulevard.

But the singer who walked onto the stage
had given their life to train solfege.
The hours alone in practice spaces
& I rolled my eyes there in boredom.

The lights went up & intermission came.
I drank a $20 glass of champagne,
grabbed my coat & stepped into the rain
& jogged a couple blocks so I could catch my train.

I breathed in deep as I burst in,
it smelled like wet dog & urine.
I checked my wallet, phone, car keys & took a seat.
Leaned my head on a window & fell asleep.
