panic is a pleasure state
existence is an escapade
your absence reverberates
and then my thoughts degenerate
youthfulness incinerates
purity intimidates
nothingness precipitates
forever on your face

desire is to be desired
feeling is just feeling tired
to love is just to be a liar
lying there like a broken wire
like an open fire
