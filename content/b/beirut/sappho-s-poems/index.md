Ran away to Eros, babe
And from off of the fire I came
Are you aware of my plan?
As you lay down in the sand
Your eyes are razor blades
My years are wasting away
So you wait in the shadowless light
So you wait in the hotel tonight
Your eyes are razor blades
My years are wasting away
