Adult PTSD is often hard to understand
What I seem to find is that in my much more wealthier clients, their mental health symptoms tend to get worse
Worse with age and much more worse with success
Understand that when you are chasing a dream, you tend to be under a false pretence that the Lamborghini or the Ferrari will make you happier
Or that the big house with the pool will make your problems just vanish and go away
What I often find in my research is that it's a lot easier to maybe face your demons in your bedroom in your mum's house
Instead of the mansion full of pain that you have now built for yourself, the unstable relationship with your partner, or the estranged relationship with your family and friends that you have now built becomes more apparent than ever
If you add millions into the unhinged way of thinking, you almost develop a bit of an oxymoron, with the assumption they should be happy because they have attained all of this newfound success or jewellery, causing people to overlook the layers of mental damage
And this is often when intervention is needed the most (needed the most, needed the most)
